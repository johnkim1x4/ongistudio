GamLog 감록

Does GamLog measure my blood pressure?

No. GamLog doesn’t measure blood pressure and doesn’t diagnose. Take readings with your own cuff, then type the numbers in. GamLog can’t tell you what a reading means for you. If you’re unsure, contact your doctor.

Where are my readings stored?

Only on your iPhone, in GamLog’s own storage, and in your device backup. GamLog has no account, no server and no analytics, so we can’t see your readings and can’t recover them for you. Keep your iPhone backed up. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I share my readings with my doctor?

Make the one-page report from the Report tab, then print it, save it to Files, or send it by Messages or Mail. You can also export every reading as a CSV file.

I bought GamLog Pro. How do I restore it on a new iPhone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, open the Pro screen in GamLog and tap Restore purchase. Purchases are handled by Apple, so for refunds use reportaproblem.apple.com.

A reading is very high. What should I do?

GamLog will point you to guidance from the American Heart Association (or, in Korean, the KDCA). If you have symptoms such as chest pain, shortness of breath or changes in vision, call 911 (119 in Korea) right away. Don’t wait for an email reply.
