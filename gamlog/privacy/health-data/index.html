GamLog Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This policy is for consumers in Washington, Nevada and Connecticut, under Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar state laws. It covers GamLog, an iPhone app made by John Kim, who publishes apps as Ongi Studio ("we").

Consumer health data we collect, and why

GamLog stores the health information you enter or import (blood pressure, pulse, weight, notes, events such as a dose change, and medicines) so you can keep a blood pressure notebook, see charts, set reminders and make reports. It is processed only on your iPhone. We never receive it.

If you email us, you may include health information in your message. We use it only to answer you.

Where it comes from

  • You, when you type it into GamLog (on your iPhone, by Siri, or on your Apple Watch, which sends it straight to your iPhone) or write to us.
  • Apple Health, only if you turn on reading from Apple Health.
  • CSV files you choose to import.

Consumer health data we share, and with whom

We don't share or sell consumer health data, and no third parties or affiliates receive it from us. GamLog sends your data only where you send it yourself: to Apple Health, if you turn on saving there, or in a report or CSV file you share.

Support emails are stored by Google Workspace, our email provider, which handles them only on our behalf.

Your rights and how to use them

You can ask us to confirm whether we hold your consumer health data, to give you a copy, to delete it, or to withdraw your consent. Your GamLog notebook is only on your iPhone: you can see, export and delete it in the app at any time, and deleting GamLog deletes it. For anything in your emails to us, write to support@ongistudio.app. We'll reply within 10 days.

If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer. We'll respond to an appeal within 45 days, and if you disagree with the outcome you can contact your state's Attorney General.
