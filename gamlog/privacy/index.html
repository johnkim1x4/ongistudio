GamLog privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

GamLog is a blood pressure notebook for iPhone, made by John Kim, who publishes apps as Ongi Studio ("we"). It does not measure blood pressure and does not diagnose. This policy explains what happens to what you put in it. In short: your notebook stays on your iPhone, and we never receive it.

What GamLog stores on your iPhone

Everything you enter: the names of the people whose notebooks you keep, blood pressure and pulse readings, weight, notes, events such as a dose change, medicines, and your reminder and report settings. Most of this is health information, so we treat all of it as sensitive.

It is stored only on your iPhone, in GamLog's own storage, protected by your iPhone's passcode and encryption. GamLog has no account and no server, so we can't see, copy or recover it.

Your notebook is included in your iPhone's backups (iCloud Backup, or a backup on your computer) if you have backups turned on, so it comes with you to a new iPhone. Apple's privacy policy covers those backups. iCloud Backup is end-to-end encrypted only if you turn on Advanced Data Protection; otherwise Apple holds the keys. GamLog doesn't sync to iCloud.

What we receive

  • Nothing from the app. GamLog has no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code that sends data anywhere, and it doesn't track you.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics, only if you've chosen to share them with app developers in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements. Apple sends these to us without your name or your readings.
  • Your emails, if you write to us. We get your email address and whatever you include, and use them only to answer you.

Apple Health

Only if you turn it on, GamLog reads blood pressure and pulse readings from Apple Health, saves the readings you type in GamLog to Apple Health, or both. You choose what to allow in the Health app and can change it at any time. Data from Apple Health stays on your iPhone: it's used only for your notebook, never for advertising, and never sent to us or anyone else.

Apple Watch, widgets and Siri

If you use the GamLog Apple Watch app, readings you take on the watch go straight from your Apple Watch to your iPhone over Apple's own connection between the two, and are saved in your notebook there. Your iPhone sends the watch your notebooks' names, today's morning and evening readings and your latest reading, so the watch can show them. Nothing goes through iCloud or to us.

Widgets read your notebook on your iPhone; Lock Screen widgets and watch faces show only whether a reading is due, never the numbers. When you log a reading with Siri, it's saved on your iPhone like any other.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. Their text names the morning or evening sitting, never your readings.

Reports and CSV files

A PDF report or CSV file is made only when you ask for one, and goes only where you send it: for example to your doctor by Messages or Mail, to Files, or to a printer. From then on, the app or service you sent it with handles it under its own privacy policy.

Purchases

GamLog Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; GamLog only learns whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account. We never see your payment details.

How long things are kept

  • Your notebook: on your iPhone until you delete it. Deleting a person in GamLog deletes their readings, events and medicines. Deleting GamLog deletes everything it stored on your iPhone. Copies in earlier backups remain until those backups are replaced.
  • Support emails: until your question is answered, then deleted within 12 months of your last message, unless the law requires us to keep them longer.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics: for as long as Apple makes them available to us.

Selling and sharing

We don't sell or share personal information, including for targeted advertising, and we don't use sensitive personal information to infer anything about you. Support emails are stored by Google Workspace, our email provider, which handles them only on our behalf.

Your rights

Your notebook is in your hands: you can view, edit, export (as CSV) and delete it in the app. For anything we hold about you, which is only support emails, you can ask us to show it to you, correct it or delete it by writing to support@ongistudio.app. We'll reply within 10 days, and we won't treat you differently for asking.

Depending on where you live, laws such as the California Consumer Privacy Act and similar state laws, or Korea's Personal Information Protection Act, give you these rights. If you aren't happy with our answer, you can complain to the privacy regulator where you live.

UK, EU and EEA users

Under the UK and EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), John Kim (Ongi Studio) is the controller of the little personal data we receive. You can reach us at support@ongistudio.app.

  • Support emails: we use them to answer you, which is in our legitimate interest and yours. If you include health information, we use it only because you chose to send it, and only to answer you.
  • Crash reports and usage statistics: Apple asks your consent before sharing them; we use them only to fix problems in GamLog.
  • Your notebook isn't something we process at all: it never leaves your iPhone except where you send it.

Google Workspace stores support emails and may do so outside the UK and EU, including in the United States, under Google's data processing terms, which include the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We don't make automated decisions about you.

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our use of your personal data, to receive a copy of it, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also complain to your data protection authority: in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office; in the EU or EEA, the authority in the country where you live.

Consumer health data (Washington, Nevada and Connecticut)

These states have their own laws about consumer health data. See our Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy.

Children

GamLog isn't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. A parent can keep a notebook for a child; it stays on the iPhone like everything else.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. If GamLog would ever start sending your information anywhere (for example, to sync between devices), we'll ask you in the app first.

Contact
