GamLog privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

GamLog keeps a blood pressure notebook on your iPhone. It does not measure blood pressure and does not diagnose.

What GamLog stores

The readings, notes, events and medicines you enter, and the reminders you set. They are stored only on your iPhone, in GamLog's own storage, and are included in your device backup.

What GamLog sends anywhere

Nothing. GamLog has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. It does not track you.

Apple Health

Only if you turn it on, GamLog reads blood pressure and pulse readings from Apple Health and/or saves the readings you type into Apple Health. You choose which data to allow in the Health app and can change it at any time. Data from Apple Health is never used for anything else and never leaves your iPhone through GamLog.

Reports and CSV files

A report or CSV file is created only when you ask for one, and goes only where you send it (for example, to your doctor by Messages or Mail, or to a printer).

Purchases

GamLog Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment; GamLog only sees whether Pro is unlocked.

Deleting your data

Deleting a person in GamLog removes their readings from the app. Deleting GamLog removes everything it stored.

Contact

Email support@ongistudio.app. GamLog is made by Ongi Studio.
