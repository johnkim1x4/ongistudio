온기

ongi · on-gee · Korean, noun

  1. Warmth; the gentle heat of something cared for.
  2. The feeling that someone has looked after a place: a floor warmed by ondol on a winter morning, a lamp left on for you.

Why “Ongi”?

We wanted a name for the feeling good everyday software should leave behind. Not dazzling or clever: quiet, dependable, and a little kinder than it strictly needs to be.

So each app starts with a small, real problem, like settling a shared bill without the awkward follow-up or keeping a parent’s blood pressure log for the doctor, and we keep at it until it feels warm in your hand.
